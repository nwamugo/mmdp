Pillar One (Returnee Resettlement and Re-integration)

Introduction

The key objective of this pillar is to enable the dignified reception and reintegration for all returnees to Edo State; promote safe voluntary return of migrants and explore migration agreements with key destination countries. This is to ensure that returnees are properly resettled to reduce the possibility of further irregular migration and re-trafficking.

What we are doing

The Edo State Government, through its Anti-Trafficking Taskforce have received and supported thousands of returnees between November, 2017 to date. Providing Temporary housing; Stipends; Skill Acquisition programs; Medical Screening and treatment as well as collecting and collating necessary data that informs the work we do. 10,000 or more returnees are expected to be resettled in Edo State in 2018 alone. So far, the State Government has invested over €650,000 into the Returnee Resettlement and Re- integration Programme. In order to ensure the proper rehabilitation of returnees and to prevent further irregular migration and re-trafficking, the state requires urgent support to be able to cater for the expected influx of returnees through the outlined activities.

Key Activities

  • Setup of a returnee information management system / database
  • Strengthened returnee reception through the provision of:
    • Welcome pack with basic necessities
    • Short-term shelter
    • Medical and psychological screening
    • Family tracking and reuniting
  • Build a world-class returnee shelter
  • Provision of 3-month stipend for all returnees
  • Security screening and profiling of returnees
  • Assisted voluntary return for migrants abroad
  • Review existing bilateral agreements on migration and explore new agreements with destination countries
