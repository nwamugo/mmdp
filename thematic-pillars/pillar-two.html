Pillar Two (Reducing Irregular Migration)

Introduction

The main objective of this pillar is to enhance the economic empowerment of youths and returnees for the purpose of reducing the economic pressures which drives migration to Europe and other destination countries. We will achieve these through;

  • Providing world-class training to residents of Edo in line with employers’ demands, and connecting them with career development opportunities.
  • Designing programmes to promote entrepreneurship through technology, incubating and accelerating 10,000 businesses in 3 years.
  • Revamping technical vocational and education through the rehabilitation and refurbishment of 4 TVETColleges, 1 Agricultural College and 18 Vocational Skill Centres to develop the skills of youth.

What we are doing

Skill Development and Employability

The Edo State government has invested in a state-wide registration of unemployed youths which yielded over 200,000 registrations. Based on this, Edojobs was developed to create jobs and economic empowerment for youths.
The primary beneficiaries of these program are those who are most susceptible to illegal migration and human trafficking. The State Government has already invested in the state-wide registration exercise, an automated job portal, job fairs, training, rehabilitation of skill acquisition centres and job matching programmes.

Promoting Technical Vocational Education and Training

TVET reforms are being implemented in Edo State to up-skill young people in order to give them access to good jobs. An analysis of the existing Technical and Vocational Institutions have been carried out and State Government is investing $12 million in the rehabilitation and refurbishment of the Government Science and Technical College, Benin. 3 Colleges of Agriculture had to be shutdown in order to be renovated, upgraded and reopened.

Key Activities

  • Job Matching: Automated online job portal and job fairs
  • Foster partnerships between higher institutions and relevant industries to align curriculum to industry needs and improve uptake of graduates
  • Employability programmes will train individuals on soft and technical skills
