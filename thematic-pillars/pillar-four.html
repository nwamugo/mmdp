Pillar Four (Provide Quality Public Services in At-risk Communities)

Introduction

The Objective of this pillar is to explore and expand programmes that enhance the delivery of basic social services and social protection to citizens and returnees. It is aimed at improving the overall quality of life and security of Edo State residents who live in the poorest communities where most of the returnees and trafficked victims come from. Data from returnees highlights that people from specific communities are much more likely to participate in irregular migration than others. At the moment, some infrastructures are moribund with outdated working tools and equipment; Short fall in human capacity; No formal and organized social protection system in the State; Few rural communities have access to safe water supply etc.

What we are doing

The Edo State Government is doing the following;

  • Implementing an Accelerated Rural Development Program to provide basic services and improve quality of life in rural communities.
  • Investing in the improvement of primary healthcare delivery in 25 LGAs with a view to expanding the impact across the state.
  • Transforming basic education through technology for learning and for teaching . The program being implemented with Bridge International Academies has kicked off with a pilot programme in 300 Primary schools with the training of 2,000 teachers and provision of devices.

Key Activities

  • Increase access to Quality Education Services for all children. This is to be provided through instruments aimed at increasing coverage and school enrolment in under- serviced communities
  • Quality Primary Health care services provided to reduce morbidity and mortality especially among infants, children and mothers
  • Access to safe, clean drinking water and sanitation services to be provided
  • Increase access to modern energy service through the state.
  • Establish an Aid Information Management System (AIMS) to track donor funded activities across the State
  • Improvement of Water Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH) facilities
