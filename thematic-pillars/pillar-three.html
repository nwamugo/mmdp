Pillar Three (Eradicating Human Trafficking)

Introduction

The main objective of this pillar is to enable targeted programmes geared towards tackling all forms of human trafficking/modern slavery from Edo State through the 4Ps of protection, prevention, prosecution and partnership.

What we are doing

The Governor inaugurated an Anti-Trafficking Taskforce in August 2017 to oversee the implementation of the State’s strategy to eradicate human trafficking from Edo State. The Taskforce comprises of diverse advisory members and stakeholders addressing trafficking in Edo State including the relevant Federal Agencies. Through the Anti-Trafficking Taskforce, we are addressing the issue of human trafficking by utilising a 4P approach of Prevention, Protection, Prosecution and Partnership. The data collected from migrant returnees shows that some returnees were either trafficked, on the verge of being trafficked, or migrants who became trafficked in the course of emigration. Trafficked victims have benefitted from immediate response by the State Government to reintegrate and rehabilitate them into the society.
The data collected so far provides concrete information with the potential to bring down the trafficking rings if properly utilised. Over 20 arrests of traffickers have been made so far and investigations are still ongoing for more arrest. The Edo State Government has also passed a new law against Human Trafficking to strengthen law enforcement. A number of antitrafficking sensitization programs have been ongoing across Edo State through town hall meetings , grass roots sensitization, churches, mosques and communities especially in local areas most affected by human trafficking. The Oba of Benin’s Palace continues to engage with the State in the area of advocacy.

Key Activities

  • Enhancing Law Enforcement and Justice

    To Strengthen Justice Delivery through the provision of infrastructure and equipment;

    • Renovation of court rooms
    • Provision of equipment for effective justice delivery
    • Establishment of the Citizens Rights Centre
    • Creation of data base of criminal cases in the State
    • Provision of vehicles for State prosecutors to aid effective prosecution of criminal cases
    • Establish international cooperation on cracking down human trafficking networks with the Interpol
    • Create a whistle blower policy and establish a witness protection system
  • Build Institutional Capacity on Antitrafficking

    To improve investigation and prosecution capacity;

    • State Judicial Library updated with relevant materials
    • Updating the Ministry of Justice Library
    • Rehabilitation and refurbishment of courts
    • Improved Trafficker Identification
    • Rehabilitation and Construction of Prisons
    • Enhancing investigative capacity
  • Promoting Human Rights and Supporting Victims

    To improve investigation and prosecution capacity;

    • Public Awareness of Citizens Rights
    • Victim Identification and support
    • Develop a human trafficking identification mechanism manual for Edo State
    • Medical support
    • Welfare support
    • Psychosocial support
  • Advocacy and Awareness raising

    To strengthen advocacy programmes and awareness:

    • Sensitization workshops on migration and human trafficking
    • Roadshows
    • Town hall meetings
    • Media campaigns
    • Produce Documentaries
    • Produce TV and radio programmes
