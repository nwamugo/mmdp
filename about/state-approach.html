The Edo State Approach

The present government of Edo State led by Governor Godwin Obaseki since coming to power in 2016, has set out a clear agenda and vision of developmental transformation as a pathway to addressing critical deficiencies in the social and economic sectors of the State. A development strategy has been formulated around six thematic pillars, namely;

Institutional Reform Infrastructural Development
Social Welfare Enhancement Environmental Sustainability
Economic Revolution Culture & Tourism

Long-term prosperity of the state will depend on the targeted interventions of these six pillars. It is expected that the resulting economic prosperity and opportunities generated from this strategic plan will significantly impact on the agenda to reducing irregular migrations and trafficking in Edo State. This will be the first most comprehensive sub-national response to the challenge of irregular migration and human trafficking in Nigeria. A significant momentum was created when Governor Godwin Obaseki unveiled a government led integrated approach to tackling the push and pull factors behind irregular migration. The Edo State Government recognises that the problem of irregular migration and human trafficking is part of a larger developmental issue in Edo State.Through an integrated approach, the Edo State Government have resolved to mainstream the eradication of human trafficking and irregular migration in its overall development strategic plan as described earlier. The idea is to refrain from isolating the issue but rather, to enable and promote a more sustainable way to tackle the challenge. The Managing Migration through Development programme is therefore formulated through a development lens, where development is a key factor in addressing the current migration crises and more importantly, in preventing future irregular migration from the state. It is against this backdrop that the four thematic pillars have been set out as the key objectives of this programme.
