Coordination

Actualising the vision and objective of this programme cannot be possible without the needed partnership with key stakeholders at international, national, sub-national and grassroots levels. However, for any level of collaboration to be possible and sustainable, we have to ensure an efficient coordination of efforts.

What We Are Doing

The State has taken the lead in mobilizing key stakeholders through adhoc meetings, technical roundtables, workshops and conferences to enable the following

Exchange of information Bilateral and multilateral collaborations Establish linkages
Reduce duplication of efforts Value for money Explore new areas of collaborations
Enhance international support Advancement of project outcomes

One of the most recent technical roundtable organised by the State was held in Abuja on the 23rd of May 2018. The roundtable which was attended by over 130 participants from Ministries, Departments and Agencies (MDAs) and International Partners, led to very clear outcomes for the Managing Migration through Development programme. Most significantly, it led to the following outcomes;

01

Collaborations with key international stakeholders who are now exploring ways to work with the State to actualise the short-term targets across the 4 thematic pillars.

02

The European Union delegation in Nigeria is playing a major role in enhancing partnerships and coordination for this programme. The EU remains a major partner in this programme following its numerous projects closely related to the 4 thematic pillars.

03

Through the Edo State Anti -trafficking Taskforce, we are overseeing the national coordination of national and local partners including The National Agency for Prohibition of Trafficking in Persons (NAPTIP), The Nigerian Immigration and other relevant MDAs. We work closely with these stakeholders to ensure a more participatory approach to achieving our overall strategy.

04

Coordination Mechanism: One of the major take away from the technical roundtable was the need to establish a mechanism to coordinate the “managing migration through development programme” given the diversity of key stakeholders potentially involved in the programme and to enable synergy in collaboration. This has led to the creation of a Steering Committee for the Managing Migration through Development Programme. The Steering Committee is led by Edo State Government, the EU and World Bank with other stakeholders as members and observers.
