Objective of the Steering Committee

The main objective of the Steering Committee is to coordinate, advise, guide and support the actualisation of the objectives of the Managing Migration through Development Programme which is set against the aforementioned thematic pillars. The Steering Committee will promote the following;

Advance the collaboration of MDA’s and International Partners within the programme
Monitor and evaluate the progress of the programme
Foster international cooperation through bilateral /multilateral networking
Enable synergy in collaboration amongst stakeholders
Promote information exchange and sharing
